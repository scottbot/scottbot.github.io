{{- /* The house ampersand, one implementation for all three callers: every "&" set in italic, per the colophon — they look swooshy and neat. Input: an HTML string (already rendered or escaped). Two shapes are caught: freestanding "&" (and "&c."), and letter-tight ampersands between capitals (Texas A&M, ASIS&T). Ampersands inside URLs and attributes are left alone — no CV data contains a URL with "&", and the guards would skip most anyway. */ -}} {{- $html := printf "%s" . -}} {{- $html = replaceRE `(^|[\s(>])&(\s|c\.|$)` `$1&$2` $html -}} {{- $html = replaceRE `([A-Z])&([A-Z])` `$1&$2` $html -}} {{- $html | safeHTML -}}
