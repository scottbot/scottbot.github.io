{{- /* Inline markdown → HTML, with the house ampersand. Every ampersand is set in italic, per the colophon: they look swooshy and neat. Three shapes are caught: freestanding "&" (and "&c."), a lone "&" that is the whole string (section headings), and letter-tight ampersands between capitals (Texas A&M, ASIS&T). Ampersands inside URLs and attributes are left alone — no CV data contains a URL with "&", and the guards would skip most anyway. */ -}} {{- $html := markdownify . -}} {{- $html = replaceRE `(^|[\s(>])&(\s|c\.|$)` `$1&$2` $html -}} {{- $html = replaceRE `([A-Z])&([A-Z])` `$1&$2` $html -}} {{- $html | safeHTML -}}
