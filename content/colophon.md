My human co-authors and I wrote all prose on this site. So-called "AI" contributed in three ways: 
designing and composing the site, converting my old blog posts and published works to site-ready pages, 
and creating the faux printer's device at the bottom of my [curriculum vitae](/cv/#colophon). 
I reviewed everything, though in the spirit of brutalist architecture (and honesty) I 
allowed some of its peculiarities and pathologies to bleed through in the design and the (non-minified) code.

For sustainability purposes, the pages themselves are built with the static-site generator Hugo. 
I mostly only edit markdown and YAML, and Claude handles the rest. The site's design is inspired
by my frankly silly [printed cv](/cv.pdf): EB Garamond font, simple colors, 
and occasional old-fashioned ligatures. No trackers, cookies, or analytics are present. 
In the grand tradition, new blog posts may be tracked via [RSS](/blog/index.xml).
